How our playbook is made

Our guides should help you understand and play this edition of the imposter game.

Rules, suggestions and examples

Rules pages describe Alibi’s implementation. Strategy tips, group sizes and clue examples are suggestions, not guarantees of winning or evidence from a controlled study. Other imposter games may use different voting, guessing or elimination rules.

Authorship and AI assistance

Imposter Play publishes the guides and is the point of accountability for corrections. AI assistance has been used to create the initial game code and draft guide text and word packs. We do not present these pages as independently reviewed expert articles or invent individual authors, qualifications or playtesting histories.

Word-pack checks

Automated tests check that the built-in records are unique, complete and assigned to real categories. They also check that Undercover excludes entries without an alternative. These checks cannot establish whether a hint feels fair, funny or understandable to every group. Choose vocabulary everyone knows and report problematic pairs.

Sources and updates

The game itself is the reference for our rules. Clue examples are illustrative. If future articles discuss another product or make externally verifiable claims, they should identify the relevant sources and distinguish observation from opinion. We do not change dates solely to make unchanged content appear new.

Corrections

Use our feedback channel to flag errors. Confirmed substantial corrections belong in the release history. Published testing results and outstanding limitations are described in testing notes.
