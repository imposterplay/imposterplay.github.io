What has been tested

These notes describe checks performed on 25 October 2026. They are a record of this release, not a certification.

Automated checks: 10 passing tests

  • Four text-contrast checks: primary button, orange role card, muted text and floating button.
  • Word records are unique, complete and use valid categories.
  • Words do not repeat before the selected pool is exhausted.
  • Undercover excludes custom entries without alternatives.
  • Imposters remain a minority, and a winning accusation must identify all imposters.
  • Shuffling preserves the input and produces a permutation.
  • Invalid player counts and empty word pools fail before dealing.

Build and browser checks

The generated HTML is checked for local links, a single main heading, descriptions and parseable structured data. On the live GitHub Pages site, the setup loaded and a round started successfully, reaching the first private-card handoff. Development checks also covered keyboard focus, narrow-screen reflow and invitation fallback behavior.

What remains unverified

A complete screen-reader audit, broad testing across physical mobile devices and systematic playtesting with real groups remain outstanding. We have not validated every word pair with players or established that our strategy suggestions improve win rates. Automated contrast checks alone do not demonstrate full accessibility compliance.

Privacy limits

Card hiding helps players share a phone; it cannot protect secrets from someone inspecting browser developer tools. Settings and word history use browser storage. Offline play depends on supported caching and an initial successful online visit. Read the privacy page for details.
