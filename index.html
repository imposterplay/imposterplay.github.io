The full rules ↗
01
▧

Peek. Don't tell.

Pass the phone. Everyone privately checks their card. Most see the same secret word. The imposter gets a hint instead.

02
〰

Make it believable.

Take turns giving one-word clues. Be specific enough to fit in, but don't give the secret away. Imposters: improvise.

03
☞

Call their bluff.

Discuss the clues, agree on your suspects, then reveal. Catch every imposter to win. Miss one and the imposters take the round.

Is Alibi really free?＋

Yes. All six word packs, both modes, custom words and group voting are free. There are no accounts or in-game ads.

Can we play on different phones?＋

This edition is made for sharing one phone in the same room. It does not connect devices or create online rooms. Share the website to invite friends to a game night.

How many people can play?＋

Bring 3–20 players. Four to eight is a great starting point. Bigger groups can add up to three imposters, with fewer imposters than civilians.

What makes Undercover different?＋

Undercover players see a related but different word, without being told their role. Clues reveal which players are working from another secret.

Does it work offline?＋

After your first visit finishes loading, the game saves its main files for offline play in supported browsers. Open it once while connected before taking it somewhere without internet.
