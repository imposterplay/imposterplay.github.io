Alibi

About Imposter Play

Imposter Play is the publishing brand behind Alibi, our free imposter word game for friends sharing one phone.

What we make

This project focuses on a game you can start in the browser: private role cards, Classic and Undercover modes, custom words and a shared final accusation. The current release supports 3–20 players in the same room. It has no remote rooms, accounts or payment features.

The person behind the game

Zeeshan Sarwar is the person behind Imposter Play and the Alibi game project. For questions, feedback or collaboration, contact Zeeshan Sarwar on LinkedIn.

The site and playbook are published under the Imposter Play brand. Our public GitHub organization identifies the publishing account, and our feedback page provides a way to report errors. We do not claim professional game-design credentials or an independent expert review.

How the game and guides are made

AI assistance is used to draft code, word-and-hint combinations and explanatory text. Automated checks validate the game rules and word records; browser checks inspect the implemented flow. The guides describe this edition’s actual rules, with illustrative clue examples. This process does not establish that every strategy or word pair has been tested with real groups.

The current collection contains 144 word, hint and alternative records created for this project. We do not use the competitor’s extracted word database or article text in the published game. Familiar game concepts and ordinary words are shared across the genre; Alibi has its own implementation and house rules.

Why this site exists

The aim is to make an in-person game easy to start and explain, with useful word ideas and clear privacy controls. We prioritize a playable game before supporting articles.
