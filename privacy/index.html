Alibi

Your secrets stay at the table.

Alibi does not ask you to create an account. This version has no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or multiplayer backend.

Saved on your device

The game stores player names, custom words, settings, word history and a round counter in your browser’s local storage. These are not sent to an Alibi server by the game. The Reset saved data button on the homepage removes this data.

Private role cards

Round roles exist in browser memory and are cleared when you leave the round. Card concealment helps with sharing a phone; it does not protect against someone using browser developer tools or inspecting the device. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Offline files

A service worker caches game files in supported browsers after the first successful visit. Clear this website’s browser storage to remove those files. Reset saved data removes game preferences but does not uninstall the offline cache.

External requests

The design requests fonts from Google Fonts, which receives ordinary connection data such as your IP address. If fonts are unavailable, the site uses system fonts. Your hosting provider also receives normal website requests. The game itself does not submit player names or words with those requests.

Sharing

Invite friends uses your device’s share sheet or copies the public website link. It does not include your player list, secret word or roles.
