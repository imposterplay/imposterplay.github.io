Report a problem

Open a website issue on GitHub

A GitHub account is required to submit an issue. You can read existing reports without posting. This is our public issue-tracking channel; we do not promise a response time.

What helps us reproduce it

  • The page address, browser and device type.
  • What you selected and what you expected to happen.
  • The error text or a screenshot with private information removed.
  • For a word correction: the word pair and why it is confusing.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post personal player information, passwords, tokens or other sensitive details. For an accessibility issue, describe the control and any assistive technology involved.

How corrections are handled

We compare reports with the implemented rules, reproduce technical issues where possible, and correct confirmed problems. Material changes are summarized in the release history. See our content policy for the limits of our advice.
