Choose a word with more than one angle

“Coffee” gives players taste, routines, cafes, cups and sleepy mornings to work with. “Thing” is too broad to support a useful clue. A highly specialized word is unfair if only one person knows what it means.

Keep hints one step removed

A hint should help an imposter join the conversation without solving the round immediately. For “pancake,” “a slow morning” gives a useful direction. A literal ingredient list would give too much away.

Make your own word pack

Open Bring your own words in the game setup. Put one entry on each line, using a vertical bar between the word, hint and optional alternative. For example:

Birthday cake | A celebration | Cupcake
Train | All aboard | Tram

Custom words join the selected built-in topics. To play only custom words, clear the topic selection. Undercover ignores entries without an alternative. Keep words short enough to remember and suitable for everyone playing.

Try a theme night

Ask each friend to contribute five words about a shared interest. Mix familiar places, everyday objects and a few surprises. Avoid personal information or clues that make one player uncomfortable. See our clue-writing guide for ways to keep the game interesting without making it confusing.
